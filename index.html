    Board

    Your lecture on screen, one line at a time, like writing on a real board.

    1. ✍️ Write in plain text. Every line you type becomes one step. # starts a slide, and maths works without LaTeX.
    2. ▶️ Present with Space or →. Each press reveals the next line, so the class follows you instead of reading ahead.
    3. 🖊️ Draw over any slide with the pen, and rub out with the duster. A clock tells you when time is up.
