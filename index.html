Board

A blank board for teaching. Opens instantly, works without internet, no login.

  1. 🖊️ Draw with a mouse, stylus or finger. Pick a colour and a size; the duster rubs out. Turn the pen off to point or rest your hand on the board without marking it. Undo brings back anything, even a cleared page.
  2. 📄 Pages. + Page gives you a fresh board. Flip with ◀ ▶, the arrow keys or a presentation clicker. Everything stays on this device, even if you close the tab.
  3. 💾 Save & share. Save turns every page into one picture, ready for WhatsApp or Teams.

Grid or ruled lines for maths · a lecture clock · chalkboard or whiteboard · install it as an app.
